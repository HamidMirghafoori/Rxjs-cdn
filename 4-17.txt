<b>connect</b>
In RxJS, the connect() operator is used in conjunction with "connectable observables." Normally, observables are cold, meaning they start emitting values when they are subscribed to. A connectable observable, on the other hand, is one that starts emitting values only when its connect() method is called.

The connect() operator, like the share() operator, converts a cold observable into a hot observable, but they differ in how they manage subscriptions:
connect() allows for manual control over when the observable starts emitting. 
share() automatically handles the subscription and connection behind the scenes.

<i>Syntax:</i>
connect(): Observable
