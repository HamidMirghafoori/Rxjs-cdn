<b>dematerialize</b>
The dematerialize() operator in RxJS is used to reverse the transformation applied by the materialize() operator. It converts a stream of Notification objects back into their original emissions (next, error, or complete).
This is useful in situations where you need to inspect or modify an observable's emission events (values, errors, or completion) and then restore the observable back to its original form.

<i>Syntax:</i>
dematerialize(): Observable
